Garagiste – Racing Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 17, 2025

🔒

Garagiste does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No account is required. No analytics are gathered. No data ever leaves your device.

Information We Collect

We collect no information whatsoever. Garagiste does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, device identifiers, or any other data from users.

Data Storage

All game data (your team, progress, and settings) is stored exclusively on your local device. This data is yours and never leaves your device unless you back it up through Apple's standard iCloud backup service, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

Garagiste does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services. There are no SDKs in the app that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Because Garagiste collects no data at all, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a feature is added that involves data collection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
