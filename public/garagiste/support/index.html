Frequently Asked Questions

The app crashes on launch

Try restarting your iPhone and relaunching the app. If the issue persists, delete and reinstall from the App Store, then contact me if it still happens.

My progress was lost

Game data is stored locally on your device. Make sure iCloud backups are enabled so your progress is preserved if you switch phones.

How do I give feedback or suggest a feature?

Send an email to the address above — all feedback is welcome and actively used to shape future updates.

How much does the game cost?

Garagiste is a paid app. You pay once and get the full game — no subscriptions, no in-app purchases.
